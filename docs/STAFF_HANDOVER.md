# Staff Shift Handover

Open **Shift Handover** under **Guest Operations**. You need your individual operational staff account.

## Outgoing staff

1. Review the four sections in Prepare Handover. Open source records when something needs attention. Check each section after reviewing it.
2. Choose the incoming active staff account. Enter the actual duty start and end in UTC and any optional notes.
3. Review the confirmation and submit. If the underlying records changed, reload and review the updated draft.
4. You can finish your handover before the next staff member acknowledges. The unacknowledged record remains visible to Eugene.

Submission saves the open items as they were at that moment. New payments or room changes do not rewrite that snapshot. Duty times do not automatically create attendance or payroll records.

## Incoming staff

Open Incoming Review and choose Show Details. Read the original open items and outgoing notes. Acknowledge after review. Comment is optional. If something differs, flag a discrepancy and describe it. Eugene records the follow-up review while retaining your original report.

Acknowledging duties does not accept cash, approve expenses or confirm that a MoMo receipt matched merchant evidence.

## Physical cash transfers

Use Cash Transfer separately when physically handing cash to another staff member. Select the recipient, enter the actual amount passed and confirm. This reserves that amount and leaves it accountable to the sender until the recipient acknowledges full receipt.

The recipient opens the transfer and either acknowledges the full amount actually received or reports a difference. A dispute stays visible to Eugene and keeps the cash reserved. Eugene's review note does not create a receipt or move custody. Once reconciled, the recipient can acknowledge with an explanation. Do not invent receipt of cash to clear an outstanding case.

After acknowledgement, Finance & Cash allows the recipient to deposit those portions or transfer available cash onward. The original receipt collector remains recorded. Cash stays due for full MoMo deposit by the original next-day deadline, including after onward transfer. Cash expenses use separate funding. There is no cash-count form.

A deposit cannot predate the recorded acquisition of that cash. When a portion of the same receipt returns to a prior custodian, the most recent acknowledgement date is the conservative earliest permitted deposit date for their remaining portion.

## Eugene's review

Owner Attention and the Owner Reports link show pending handovers, incoming discrepancies and unresolved transfers. Only the configured designated owner can review all details and record owner follow-up. Other administrators retain their existing Finance visibility but cannot read private handover snapshots through that route.

Finance & Cash → Cash Receipt Register includes Cash By Custodian, with accountable, reserved and available amounts. A custody transfer creates no sales revenue, expense, new guest receipt or MoMo deposit.

## History and exceptions

History retains the original submission, linked source facts, acknowledgements, discrepancies, reviews, staff IDs and UTC timestamps. Use Older Records to continue through the history.

A recipient who later becomes unavailable or inactive cannot be impersonated. Their pending record stays visible to Eugene. Reassignment, lost cash, write-off and amount correction require a separately evidenced authorized procedure. No history is silently edited.

This workflow uses the existing activated finance setup. No additional database activation is needed locally. See [verification and recovery evidence](delivery/changes/2026-11-14-shift-handover.md).
