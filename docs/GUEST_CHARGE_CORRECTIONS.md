# Guest Charge Corrections & Refunds

Open a **Guest Stay Account → Show Details → Correct Charge / Review Refund**, or **Finance & Cash → Financial Corrections → Guest Charges & Refunds**.

## Correct a charge

1. Check the guest, room, stay and original charge. Choose the actual charge; do not create a replacement sale.
2. Enter the supported correct **total** charge, not the difference. Explain the error. Supporting PDF/JPEG/PNG evidence up to 2 MB is optional and private to requester and configured finance owner.
3. Review the exact change to charges, revenue and guest balance. Receipts, stock quantities and actual money remain unchanged. Submit for owner review.
4. Eugene independently verifies the original evidence, then approves once, returns for revision or rejects. A returned request retains all earlier proposals and evidence. The requester can withdraw an unposted request.
5. A posted adjustment stays linked to the original charge. Use its adjustment notice for the guest; the notice excludes private reasons/files and is not a payment or refund confirmation.

Room accommodation totals can be corrected only after the final bill exists: an active stay's nights still change. Fulfilled extras and approved incidentals can be corrected during or after a stay. Unknown historical rates/dates, unsupported final bills, unresolved departure incidents, inconsistent line totals or fractional-pesewa historical lines require separate evidence reconciliation. No rate or actor is guessed.

Original charges, rate/price/quantity snapshots, stock movements, payment receipts, final departure evidence and closed-period snapshots remain retained. A correction does not change checkout dates, return stock, create a receipt, grant credit or write off debt. A real increase after departure remains due through the existing owner recovery workflow.

## Record an actual refund separately

Only the configured finance owner can open and submit **Record Actual Completed Refund**. An administrator role alone does not grant this authority.

- Independently confirm genuine excess money belongs to the guest. A recording correction or uploaded document alone is not proof of overpayment.
- Select the original receipt; enter the amount, actual **Cash** or **MoMo** method, actual payment time in UTC, actual payer, completed payment reference/evidence and reason. Confirm that the money was already returned. This form records the payment; it does not send money.
- Refunds cannot exceed current guest credit or the remaining effective original receipt after prior refunds. Corrected/compensated receipts retain their lower bounds and source links.
- Actual refunds from an earlier closed period post in today's open period with their original time/date retained. The actual return cannot precede its evidenced original collection. Original collection time and later recording time remain separate for late receipts.
- Cash refunds use separate funding. All gross cash-sales receipts still require their full deposit; expenses/refunds never reduce that obligation. Refunds are separate outflows, not another charge reduction or expense.

The guest account shows actual method, time, payer and authenticated recorder. Historical missing details remain **Not Recorded / Historical Method Unknown**. It does not fill in a guessed payer or time.

## Review and retries

Owner Reports links pending charge requests. Finance revenue/profit and extras reports include linked adjustments by posting date. Collection totals remain separate; actual refunds appear in Payment Log with their real method. Itemized statements retain original charges and show adjustments separately; current account totals include each once. Earlier closed totals and earlier as-of views stay fixed.

A source, receipt, refund, charge or guest-balance change can invalidate pending approval. Return for revision and check the refreshed preview. One source cannot have competing active requests. Persistent operation keys, source uniqueness and the shared transaction protect critical submissions and concurrent writers. A failed required audit rolls back all financial effects.

If a submission is interrupted, retry with the original action identity and identical details/evidence file. Do not invent a second request to compensate for uncertainty. A confirmed retry returns the original result. No financial history is deleted to undo a mistake; use a new supported linked correction.

This is an implemented local workflow. It requires existing occupancy/finance setup, configured owner identity and protected evidence storage. No new schema activation is required. Production deployment and parity checks require separate authorization.
