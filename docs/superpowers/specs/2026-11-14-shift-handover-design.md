# Approved Staff Shift Handover

Owner approved tivoli-shift-handover.html on 14 November 2026. Outgoing staff review four sections (guests/accounts, Cash/MoMo, rooms/faults, incidents/pending work), enter actual duty times, choose an active incoming account and submit optional notes with an immutable snapshot. Incoming acknowledgement can follow later and optionally flags a discrepancy. Eugene reviews exceptions. Original facts and each actor/time remain visible. Unknown historical facts remain unknown.

Physical cash transfer is separate. Sender confirms amount passed; recipient acknowledges full receipt or disputes. Pending/disputed amounts remain accountable to sender but reserved against deposit and other transfer. Acknowledgement moves custody once without changing original collector, receipt, gross deposit amount or next-day deadline. No physical cash-count workflow. Expense payments remain separately funded. Neither duty acknowledgement nor transfer creates sales, costs, deposits, approvals or MoMo matches. Eugene records cash review without fabricating custody changes. Recipient can acknowledge previously disputed cash with a reconciliation explanation. Amount corrections/write-offs remain outside this scope.

Use existing transactional event/operation stores, current permissions and designated-owner binding, exact minor units and shared lock. Sender/recipient/owner-only details, append-only review history and older-history navigation. Owner alerts are in-app. Freeze source tokens to reject stale submissions. Actual duty times entered by staff, not inferred from HR schedules. UTC display dd/mm/YYYY HH:MM:SS. No new schema, roles or dependencies, ordinary-database fixtures, deployment, historical rewrites or Git-state changes. Reuse existing checkout containing approved uncommitted work.
