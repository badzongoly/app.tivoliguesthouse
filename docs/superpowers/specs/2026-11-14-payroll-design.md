# Approved Payroll Review and Staff Payments
Owner approved tivoli-payroll-review.html for implementation. Eugene prepares/revises and approves monthly payroll without a fabricated second administrator. Preparation posts no cost. Approval freezes gross, net, employer cost, HR salary revision and masked MoMo destination, and records staff cost once. Record verified actual partial/full MoMo settlements separately; never pay through the app or create another expense. Publish only when employee balance is zero (including an explicitly zero-net entitlement). Staff see only their own published statement, not drafts, employer notes or evidence. Attendance/leave context never automatically reduces salary.

Use exact integer GHS minor units, existing finance_events/operations/claims/audit and shared transaction lock. Private payroll payloads use the existing HR vault. Canonical payroll_requested + request_approved and cost_paid source events preserve current finance/report totals. No new expenses row for payroll. Approved payroll is immutable; no general correction/write-off/remittance/advance workflow or tax engine. Deductions/employer contributions remain manually reviewed, with remittance explicitly not implied by employee payment.

One staff/month claim shared with legacy payroll prevents duplicate drafts/costs. Existing legacy requests/approvals/payments stay unchanged and readable. Retire public old creation. Legacy new review/payment actions require the configured owner (who can approve their own old request); provider authority remains unchanged. New linked payroll cannot settle/review through old pay_cost/request_review. Existing open legacy full settlement remains supported; old locked unreviewed costs need separate evidenced reconciliation, not backdating or reopening here.

Prepare from latest monthly verified MoMo salary revision effective <=min(service-month end,today), never a future-effective salary. Revalidate salary/profile/provider token at preparation and approval; no proration guessed for midmonth changes. Future scheduled exits remain eligible until due; new approval after exit/suspension is denied. Approved debts and history stay payable/readable independently of later staff eligibility. Freeze payee/last four digits; changing HR later does not rewrite approved destinations. An actual wrong-destination correction remains separately authorized work.

Approval/payment post to today's open period, retaining service month and actual payment timestamp; no future/backdated approval. Payment must follow actual approval and be <=now. Earlier closed totals fixed. Reference claims use shared business_momo namespace. Pending prepared costs appear in weekly/monthly review. Publish snapshots authorized employee account at publication; later HR relinking cannot transfer statements silently. Require a verified linked account before publication. Evidence downloads remain owner-only, including after publication.

Existing salary-authorized finance viewers keep authorized common financial amount/source rows with sensitive payload redacted; aggregate reports remain available under existing permissions. Private workflow/files require configured owner plus hr.salary; employee statement requires hr.self, exact publication recipient and current binding. Inactive accounts cannot read. No new role grants/secrets/migration. Existing owner/HR/finance configuration required.

Execute inline in current checkout preserving extensive preexisting uncommitted source. No Git branch/staging/commit/push, ordinary application database tests or production action. Dynamic verification only exact marked synthetic database/service. One independent final source review required.
