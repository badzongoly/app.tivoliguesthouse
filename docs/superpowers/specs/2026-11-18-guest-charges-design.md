# Guest Charge Corrections & Refunds

Approved scope: workflow 3 of the owner-reviewed ten-workflow prototype, explicitly requested for implementation. Reuse Guest Stay Account and Financial Corrections. Staff select an actual room/extra/incidental charge, propose its supported correct total, and explain the error. The configured finance owner approves, returns or rejects. Posting appends a current-open-period debit/credit, retaining original charge/source/actor/stock/checkout/closed snapshots. Charge correction never changes a receipt or returns money.

Alternatives: editing original charges is rejected because it erases history; a generic refund/negative payment is rejected because charges and actual cash movements differ. Selected approach is source-linked requests and one owner approval, matching existing corrections. The already approved prototype is the design authority; no repeated approval gate.

Engineering boundaries: accommodation is a finalized aggregate charge, not an editable current tariff. Require a known frozen departure for room corrections; active room charges continue to grow and remain unfinalized. Fulfilled extra lines and approved incidentals are independently correctable, including during active stays. Unknown rates, unsupported unfrozen historical departures, pending/disputed departure incidents and inconsistent source/bill evidence block posting. Do not infer historical line identities or return stock automatically.

Exact integer-minor amounts and before/after guest balance, revenue/profit effect, unchanged receipts and zero actual money movement appear before approval. Preserve earlier proposals/private evidence. One active source request, persisted operation keys/fingerprints, shared security-state lock, fresh source/bill versions and atomic required audit prevent stale/double effects.

Actual refunds remain the existing refund action, expanded to Cash/MoMo and restricted to the configured owner before both new posting and replay. Explicitly attest actual completed payment; capture evidenced actual time/payer/reference separately from authenticated recorder/posting time. Retain original receipt and bound by current guest credit and remaining effective original receipt after prior refunds. Claim outgoing proof once; actual past activity posts in today's open period with retained original time/date. Gross cash-sales deposits are never reduced by cash refunds. Unknown historical refund methods remain unknown; existing finance-recorded refunds retain evidenced MoMo metadata.

No new schema/main menu, provider API, tax policy, write-off, rates change, inventory return, guest credit policy or production activation/deployment. Current account, checkout cursor, statement/export, recovery, Finance profit and Owner Reports must agree. Show charge adjustments separately from original sales and refunds; old closed periods and as-of cutoffs remain fixed.

Verify guarded synthetic integration, concurrency, rollback, source immutability, unknown/refund/replay authority, active-extra checkout cursor, closed-period posting, report/as-of, private evidence, HTTP and responsive browser paths. Independent source review required before handoff.
