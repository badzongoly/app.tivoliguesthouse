# Purchase & Provider Corrections

Approved workflow 4 of the ten-workflow prototype, reaffirmed by the owner's Go ahead after comparing purchase versus ordinary-expense corrections. Reuse the original Purchase Request / Provider record and the existing Financial Corrections tab. One requester form, exact preview and one configured-owner decision; no new main menu or repeated design approval.

Correct supported recorded cost, one specifically identified completed payment, or an evidenced single-item stock receipt/actual return. Keep original approvals/caps, delivery declarations, costs, payouts, references, actors, stock movements and closed snapshots. Cost/payment differences remain supplier payable or recorded supplier recovery due; never force equality, delete a real payout or invent returned money. Existing partial settlements use the effective remaining balance after corrections.

Use append-only pc_posted adjustments over existing finance_events with durable finance_operations keys, shared AccessService transaction/lock, fresh source/payment/stock versions, private optional evidence and atomic required audit. Source original cost and specific payout are immutable; current position is a projection. Ordinary Expense Corrections routes linked purchases to this workflow and cannot independently correct them.

Modern purchases include expense, service-provider and single-item stock purchases. Legacy approved provider requests retain their original expense-free provider cost basis, financial IDs and recorded settlement. Unknown historical actual payer/payment evidence blocks payment correction; cost-only correction may preserve those unknowns and show recorded rather than independently verified supplier position. Do not guess historical stock/source mappings.

Stock receipt correction changes the supported total originally received, retaining a separate cumulative actual-return quantity. Actual returns are additional evidenced completed returns, bounded by net received quantity and current available stock. Cost is supplied explicitly from independent evidence; no inferred unit valuation or automatic FIFO/COGS. Preserve the original in-movement; append a linked adjustment or actual out-movement and update expected-stock cache atomically with cost/decision/audit. Never return stock just because cost changed. Missing links, depleted/unsupported quantities and stale stock require review. Quantities/costs beyond existing approved scope or already accepted exception remain blocked pending separate authority; approval is not backdated.

Actual supplier monetary recovery is separate from this recording correction. Keep any recovery due visible in the original source, handover and owner attention; no fake recovery, Other Income or automatic balance clearing. Follow-up notes may retain evidence without changing money. Actual remaining payments use the existing purchase payment action, or the linked owner-only provider settlement action when needed; completed-payment evidence, actual payer/time, source bounds and unique MoMo proof remain mandatory. Cash costs keep separate funding and never reduce full cash-sales deposits.

Alternative rejected: generic negative expense or editing original expense/payment/stock rows. Selected typed source-specific correction reconciles linked cost, supplier balance and stock without duplicate recognition. No new schema, role, tax policy, provider API, financial history rewrite, production migration/activation or deployment.

Acceptance: amount/category report effects in posting period; unchanged old period/as-of; correct partial payable/recovery due; source/movement/actor/reference preservation; permissions/private evidence; retries/races/rollback; old payout writer cannot bypass corrected source; inventory sale/count overlap; responsive request/owner/source screens. Independent source review before handoff.
