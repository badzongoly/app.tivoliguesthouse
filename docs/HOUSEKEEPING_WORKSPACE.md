# Housekeeping in Check-In & Occupancy

Open **Check-In & Occupancy → Room Board**. Use the **Room Status** list to find rooms that need cleaning, are being cleaned, or await inspection.

- **Start Cleaning:** confirm the assignment on the Room Board to open the first checklist step directly.
- **Continue Cleaning:** complete the captured checklist steps. Select All only checks items; it does not record completion. Save & Return retains progress.
- **Inspect & Release Room:** an authorized colleague inspects the completed room. A failed inspection returns it for cleaning. Self-inspection still requires the existing owner exception and follow-up.
- **Owner Review:** the configured owner can review inspection exceptions and required follow-ups from the Room Board controls.
- **Cleaning History:** staff see their own attempts; administrators retain the complete permitted history and can open Activity & Evidence on the record.

A completed **Guest Stay Account** includes **Room Preparation** when a cleaning attempt is explicitly linked to its finalized departure. **View Cleaning Record** opens that stay's read-only record. It never substitutes the room's latest attempt after another guest uses the room. Historical or incident preparation without an evidenced finalized-departure link remains unlinked; no association is guessed.

Current room work is always managed from the Room Board. Critical faults, occupied rooms, stale preparation cycles, assigned-cleaner checks and inspection controls remain enforced. The separate Housekeeping sidebar item is removed. Existing saved housekeeping URLs remain supported, and their actions return to the consolidated workspace. No database activation is required.

## Consolidated navigation — 22 November 2026

The Room Board and preparation views share Cleaning History and owner-only Review controls. Use the primary Room Board tab instead of a second Back To Room Board link. An open cleaning/inspection/exception/restart form does not repeat its own entry link. Its existing submit, cancel, history and safeguards remain. Authorized guest-account readers can return from an original read-only cleaning record to its explicitly linked guest stay; room-only readers are not offered an inaccessible guest link.
