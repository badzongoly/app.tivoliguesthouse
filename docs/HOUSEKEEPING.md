# Housekeeping and Room Readiness

Open **Housekeeping** from the main menu, Property & Audits, or the room's cleaning/inspection action.

## Cleaning

1. Open the room and choose Start Cleaning. Use your own account; the attempt records you as its cleaner.
2. Work through the captured checklist one step at a time. Select All ticks every cleaning item across all steps; Clear All unticks them. Neither button records completion.
3. Confirm only work actually performed. Notes and photo/evidence are optional. PDF, JPEG or PNG evidence is limited to 2 MB.
4. Save & Return preserves checked items and opens room details. Continue Cleaning resumes the same named attempt.
5. The last step requires a separate personal-completion confirmation and Record Cleaning Complete. The room stays unavailable while awaiting inspection.

Another staff member cannot overwrite your assignment. If an assignment is abandoned, Eugene can restart it with a reason. The original remains visible and the new attempt records Eugene as the new cleaner; it does not pretend that work has been completed or assign it silently to somebody else.

Checklist settings remain under administrator Settings. Changes affect future attempts; a started attempt retains both captured checklist revisions. Rooms with only older preparation evidence require a newly recorded cleaning attempt rather than an invented historical cleaner.

## Independent inspection and rework

An authorized staff member other than the cleaner opens Inspect & Release Room.

- Record Pass or Needs Attention for every item. Select All marks all items Pass; change any failed item before deciding. The personal inspection attestation stays separate.
- All items must pass before Confirm & Release Room. Confirmation makes the unused, unblocked room Ready / Available.
- If an item fails, choose Return For Cleaning. The failed item labels are recorded; a comment or photo is optional. The room remains unavailable and a new cleaning attempt is needed.
- Cleaning, inspection and failed findings remain linked to their original attempt. A later successful attempt never replaces them.

Occupied rooms, critical faults and unsecured departure incidents prevent housekeeping release. Report unsafe conditions through the established fault process. Recording repair keeps its existing workflow; this feature does not add a separate repair inspection. If occupancy or safety evidence changes during an unfinished attempt, old checklists and grants cannot release the room. Review current facts and start a fresh attempt when preparation is required.

## Self-inspection exception

The cleaner can request a one-attempt exception with the reason an independent inspector is unavailable. Eugene alone authorizes or declines it. Authorization does not release the room.

After authorization, the same cleaner still performs the complete inspection and records the actual outcome. The grant is consumed whether the room passes or returns for cleaning; another staff member's inspection also closes any unused grant. A fresh attempt needs its own authority.

If Eugene cleaned personally, he must record an explicit owner self-inspection exception before inspecting. Completed self-inspections appear in Owner Review for follow-up, including failed inspections and attempts already followed by newer cleaning. Eugene records the follow-up outcome against the exact original inspection.

## Evidence and history

Activity & Evidence retains identities, UTC timestamps, checklist snapshots, progress, comments, owner decisions, failed results and optional files. Authorized housekeeping team members can read operational evidence; finance/report access alone does not grant room-evidence access. Downloads are logged.

Owner Reports and staff handover expose pending work in the app. They do not claim email or WhatsApp delivery. Existing room and financial history remains intact. The older public immediate-clean/inspect forms redirect here, and their submissions are denied.

This workflow creates no charge, expense, payment, stock adjustment, payroll entry or historical backfill. Do not delete an original attempt or reuse incompatible older writers. If an uncertain storage failure offers Retry Same Action, reuse that action and reselect the original evidence when requested; do not invent a second completion.
