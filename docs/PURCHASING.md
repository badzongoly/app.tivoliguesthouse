# Purchasing and Expense Requests

Open **Finance & Cash > Purchase Requests**. Use your individual account.

## Staff workflow

1. Choose New Request. Select Operating Expense, Stock Purchase or Service Provider. Describe what is needed, supplier/contact, purpose, estimate and expected Cash/MoMo method. Quote evidence is optional.
2. Submit for Eugene's approval. Approval sets a spending limit; it does not record an expense or payment.
3. Record commitment only after approval. If scope or estimate changes, request a revised decision before further commitment.
4. Record the complete delivery, actual cost and original activity date. The Goods Received / Work Completed description is optional on this form. Confirm full receipt and attach the receipt if available; otherwise explain the alternative evidence. This version supports one complete delivery, not partial deliveries.
5. If already paid, declare the actual payer, method, date and reference honestly. This is unverified until Eugene confirms it. Confirmation records the existing payment; nobody should pay it again.
6. Follow the status and Activity history. If disputed, append a clarification. The original declaration remains visible.

Only the requester and configured finance owner can read private purchase details and attachments. Quote/receipt uploads accept PDF, JPEG and PNG up to 2 MB.

## Eugene's workflow

Use Owner Review Queue to approve a cap, reject, decide a proposed revision, or review delivery and actual cost. The reason on Review Spending Approval is optional for approval and rejection. Any reason entered is kept in Activity. Review any overrun or payment predating approval as an explicit after-the-fact exception; never backdate approval. Confirming cost creates one expense. For stock purchases it also adds the delivered stock once, in the same transaction.

Review an already-paid declaration before confirming it. Otherwise use Payment to record actual full or partial Cash/MoMo settlement. Check remaining due and actual date before confirming. Each partial settlement remains visible; recording payment never creates a second expense.

Cash costs use separately funded cash. They do not reduce the requirement to deposit sales cash receipts in full by the next day. Retain original MoMo references.

## Costs without prior approval

Use Report Cost Without Prior Approval for an actual Operating Expense or Service Provider cost. Explain why approval was missing and provide original evidence. Owner acceptance is an exception, not retrospective prior approval.

For stock already received or already recorded, use a **cost-only Operating Expense**, identifying the original stock movement/count and proof in the narrative. This does not add stock or automatically link an old movement. Do not submit another stock purchase to record the same delivery.

## Dates, corrections and unavailable actions

Closed periods stay fixed. Authorized late costs and payments post into an open period while retaining their original dates. Cancellation is available only before commitment. Submitted declarations are not overwritten.

If an action reports temporary unavailability, use Retry Same Action. Preserved inputs and the same operation key protect against duplicate submissions; reselect the original attachment if requested.

Existing expense/provider/stock records remain available under their existing review rules. New public creation uses Purchase Requests. General corrections, refunds, write-offs, staff-advance reimbursement and payment before delivery are separate work. Ask Eugene to review a problem rather than entering a duplicate or deleting history.


## Linked corrections

Posted purchases now use [Purchase & Provider Corrections](PURCHASE_CORRECTIONS.md) to correct supported cost, a specific recorded payment or linked stock/actual return, retaining original records. Existing partial payments use the effective remaining balance; supplier recovery due remains visible for separate actual money recovery.
