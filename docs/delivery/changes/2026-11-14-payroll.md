## Implemented contracts

Eugene alone, through the configured finance-owner account with hr.salary, prepares, returns and approves monthly payroll. Preparation creates no financial cost. Approval freezes the effective monthly HR revision, registered payee, masked MoMo destination, manual allowances/overtime/deductions/employer costs, reason and exact gross/net/staff cost. Future salary revisions are excluded; HR/profile/provider changes require preparation again. No automatic attendance deduction, statutory calculation or salary proration is inferred.

Private payroll_draft/prepared/returned/approved/paid/published events use the existing HR vault. Existing canonical payroll_requested + request_approved record gross plus employer cost once. cost_paid records each bounded actual employee settlement, never a second expense. No payroll expenses or guest receipt rows are created. Shared staff_payroll and business_momo claims prevent duplicate staff/month sources and cross-source reference reuse. Any existing staff/month claim, including a rejected legacy source, blocks a new modern draft; no replacement source is guessed.

Actual MoMo transfers are made outside the app. Positive partial/full amounts may not exceed the approved employee balance; actual UTC transfer time must follow approval and not exceed now. Approval and payment post to today's open period and retain service month and actual transfer time. Closed financial totals remain fixed. Prepared unapproved costs enter weekly/monthly completion blockers. Approved debts remain readable and payable after staff departure; departed/suspended staff cannot receive new approval.

Publish only when employee balance is zero, including zero-net entitlement without an invented payout. The publish form names and binds the currently reviewed linked account; changes require fresh verification under the shared transaction lock. Published snapshots require exact original recipient and current HR link, so relinking does not transfer earlier statements. Employees with own-HR access see only their published statement, frozen amounts and actual payment references. Internal comments, employer costs and private evidence are excluded.

Optional PDF/JPEG/PNG payment evidence up to 2 MB uses the existing protected encrypted attachment registry. Downloads are owner-only even after publication and are audited. Existing attachment design can leave encrypted unreachable orphans after a failed transaction, without an accessible committed evidence record.

Payroll & Payslips navigation leads to Monthly Payroll, Owner Review, Staff Costs, Activity, retained Legacy Payroll and Payment Log. Exact browser preview, confirmations, focused action/validation states, retained failed-form values, PRG and stable operation keys support safe use/retry. Owner Reports shows pending payroll without decrypting private payloads. Owner Payment Log enriches actual references; other existing salary-authorized finance viewers retain redacted financial source/amount rows. Employees without salary authority do not gain common payroll ledger access.

Old public payroll creation GET redirects and POST denies409 after CSRF. Original legacy requests and settlement evidence remain unchanged. New review/settlement of old payroll requires Eugene, who may approve his own request; provider maker/checker behavior is unchanged. Linked modern payroll cannot use old request_review/pay_cost routes. Trusted old creation remains for compatibility, not a reopened public form.

## Migration, recovery and boundaries

No new schema migration, activation, role grant, secret/configuration change, SQL import, branch/stage/commit/push or production deployment. Existing HR encryption, finance setup and configured owner binding are prerequisites. History is preserved; no backfill, renumbering or guessed source links.

Before any events exist, code removal requires reverting the entire coordinated route/service/integration slice without reopening the old public writer. After events exist, use a coordinated forward repair preserving private and canonical events, claims, operations, payroll identities, files, amounts and audits. Do not roll back one writer in isolation, delete transactions, reuse references or create duplicate expenses. General posted payroll correction/reversal needs a separately approved linked compensation design.

Attendance/roster/rest coverage, casual/final pay, automatic scheduling/last-Friday prompt, advances, taxes/statutory engine, deduction/employer remittances, bank/cash payroll, payment APIs and external alerts remain separate work. Employee settlement is not evidence that withheld deductions or employer contributions were remitted. This is locally implemented and verified; production parity/deployment is not established.
