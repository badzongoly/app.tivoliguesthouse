# Owner Reports · 7 November 2026

## Scope and implementation

The owner supplied the [visual reports brief](../../TIVOLI_POS_VISUAL_REPORTS_BUILD_BRIEF.md) and requested implementation. The original brief was copied into docs and its SHA-256 matched the attachment. The local checkout is C:/xampp/htdocs/app.tivoliguesthouse on main at f43281e, with pre-existing changes. The brief supplied the design reference. A separate dashboard preview was unavailable in the checked locations.

Implemented an additive, read-only server-rendered dashboard at owner_reports.php. The Money & Review sidebar shows the link for authenticated administrators with reports.view. The endpoint independently enforces both grants and only serves GET/HEAD. Report data queries use the existing PDO connection inside a repeatable-read, read-only snapshot. Pages send no-store and omit guest names, full references and evidence. Normal authentication controls may record security audit events. Legacy routes and export contracts are available alongside the new dashboard.

The daily default is today. Room Performance, Cash & MoMo and Extras default to seven calendar days through today. The room window is capped at 31 days and the cash and extras windows at 93. The Monthly Review shows the selected month and five prior months. Detail pages contain 50 rows with stable sort and a full-range summary. Dates use Accra property time (UTC over the supported reporting range) with a half-open timestamp interval clipped to report time. Expense and deposit effective dates use their stored business date. Monetary values use SQL DECIMAL and integer pesewas.

## Data contracts and evidence gaps

| View | Authoritative records and grain | Coverage and owner decisions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Daily Overview | One row per current room. Compare rooms.status with an aggregate of active bookings; use stay_room_settings.ready_state for cleaning and readiness. Count recorded arrivals and departures by their own timestamps. Count payments.created_at plus finance_events income_recorded at receipt timestamp. Sum stay_departures.room_minor by departure timestamp and selected room population. | Excludes current rooms named or typed Service for room measures. Current state is a snapshot even when a historical report date is selected. Departure charges use saved snapshots only and do not imply earned revenue. Approval of earning rules and reliable coverage would be needed for a property-wide earned series. |
| Room Performance | One room and date, deduplicated from qualifying bookings.checkin_at and checkout_at; stay_details excludes identified short stays. Starts inclusive, departure date excluded, with the current night unelapsed. Eligible inventory uses the same current Service exclusion. | Missing historical stay types, room moves and capacity changes are visible limitations. A blank cell means no qualifying recorded stay. Historical physical and sellable occupancy require a confirmed rentable room policy, reliable effective-dated capacity/block records and an approved policy for room moves and block/stay conflicts. No current-inventory percentage proxy is displayed. |
| Cash & MoMo | One receipt per payment ID or income-recorded event ID. Include advances and legacy methods. Refund totals use stay_refunds by recorded time. MoMo buckets use source-specific receipt_matched events as of report time and the finance payment cursor. Cash custody uses post-cutover cash receipts, receipt recorder and linked deposit allocations. Separate movement summary uses deposit effective date and linked wallet confirmation. | Wallet matches are staff-recorded evidence. Older receipts can lack coverage or dates. Receipt recorder is attribution from the receipt event. Physical cash variance requires a verified count and opening balance. The current operating policy assumes recorded receipts and deposits them in full, with cash expenses funded separately. |
| Extras | One booking_extras line, by stored created_at. Round stored quantity × stored unit price half-up to a pesewa per line, matching the existing Finance convention. Rank by item ID, preserve original descriptions in detail, and keep missing IDs separate. Exact stock movement links require sale ID, item ID and outgoing movement type. | Unlinked stock is a review marker with no value adjustment. A ratio needs an approved common sales and room-night scope. Unpaid, paid-by-method and reversal amounts need authoritative charge allocations and linked charge credits. Refunds stay separate. |
| Monthly Review | Monthly receipt totals use the same incoming sources by receipt timestamp. Expenses use posted expenses.expense_date and one category per expense. Categories reconcile to the selected month's posted expenses. | Separate payroll, provider and settlement events are outside the posted-expense series. Current month is to date. A comparable earned and cost basis is needed to publish a defensible operating result. |

The legacy extra-sales view still reads its first 500 lines, with the existing CSV format. The new extras report aggregates the complete date cohort before paging. No new export was added. Source links use authenticated existing GET routes.
