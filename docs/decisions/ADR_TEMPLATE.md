## Context and observed constraints

Describe the actual problem, current behaviour, supported versions, financial/security/operational constraints, and what remains unknown. Separate observed facts from assumptions.

## Decision

State the chosen design/policy, scope, source of truth, compatibility boundary, and invariants. For accounting/authority decisions, record the explicit owner decision rather than engineering assumptions.

## Alternatives and trade-offs

Compare realistic options, including keeping the existing implementation. Explain cost, failure modes, implementation burden, and why the selected option fits Tivoli.

## Financial, data, and security consequences

Record historical preservation, exact-money/reporting impact, actors/permissions/approvals, migration/cutover, idempotency/concurrency, audit/privacy, dependencies/cost, and external side effects as applicable.

## Verification and rollout

List acceptance examples, test/migration/reconciliation evidence, deployment order, monitoring/stop conditions, and recovery preserving later transactions. Record approvals separately from design acceptance.

## Open questions and revisit conditions

Name each owner, blocker, required evidence, and condition for revisiting the decision. Never record a pending proposal as an implemented control.
